# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one primary decision point:

**Primary — Brand decision-makers**: Marketing managers, brand directors, partnership directors at outdoor, lifestyle, luxury, automotive, and performance brands. They land on the site after seeing Mathis compete or being targeted by LinkedIn ads. They are evaluating ROI, not emotion. They need proof: rankings, reach, credibility. They have 90 seconds.

**Secondary — Wingfoil community and fans**: Athletes, enthusiasts, media, event organizers. They validate the brand perception that primary users rely on. A large, engaged following is the proof that converts sponsors.

## Product Purpose

mathisghio.com is a B2B sponsorship conversion asset disguised as an athlete portfolio. Its job is to make brand decision-makers want to sponsor Mathis within one visit. Success = a partnership inquiry submitted or an email sent.

## Brand Personality

Élite · Brut · Précis

The voice of someone who has nothing to prove because the results speak. Not loud, not humble — certain. Like the McLaren F1 team: performance communicated through precision, not decoration.

## References

Formula 1 team sites (McLaren, Mercedes), high-performance sports brands (Pinarello, Specialized, Garmin). What to take from them: data presented with confidence, dark environments that make performance metrics glow, editorial photography that dominates the screen, zero clutter.

## Anti-references

- Influencer lifestyle sites: too casual, loses sponsorship credibility
- Generic portfolio templates: no personality, no trust signal
- Amateur sports club sites: dated layout, wrong register entirely
- SaaS startup landing pages: too cold, loses the human and athletic identity

## Design Principles

1. **Results over narrative.** Numbers and titles before adjectives. Show the record, then explain it.
2. **Precision as personality.** Tight spacing, sharp edges, no softness. Every pixel deliberate.
3. **Dark makes performance glow.** Dark backgrounds are not aesthetic — they make the cyan stats and gold titles read as achievement, not decoration.
4. **Brand managers are buyers, not fans.** Every section should answer "why would I sponsor this?" not "isn't this cool?"
5. **Restraint is authority.** Less animation, less decoration, more signal. The GOAT doesn't need to shout.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion respected via `useReducedMotion` (already implemented). Color contrast on dark backgrounds must be verified for text over video/image backgrounds.
